Our Story

Founded in a small laboratory in Liberty City in 2026.

Eloré was born from the belief that a fragrance should never conceal, but reveal. Every scent is handcrafted in small batches in our laboratory, using only 100% carefully selected natural ingredients. We believe in the purity of nature and its ability to capture genuine emotions, creating fragrances that become lasting memories rather than fleeting impressions.
